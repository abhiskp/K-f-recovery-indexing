# Phase 6 go/no-go gate

| Gate | Decision | Evidence and missing condition |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Observed 2018 flood exposure** | **Partial, no validated event extent** | Tiwari S1 provides real georeferenced 9/21 August water-positive cells over the pilot, but shares Sentinel-1 with our candidate and lacks valid negatives/footprint. Sentinel Asia ALOS-2 is outside the pilot. The official order identifies notified districts/villages, not physical inundation. |
| **B. Post-flood agricultural or ecological recovery measurements** | **Agricultural observations available; recovery duration not established** | Phase 3 has district-season rice area, production and yield through 2019-20. Another year is also affected by the 2019 flood. No field-level trajectory, independent ecological time series or estimated recovery duration was produced. |
| **C. Geographically matched exposure–recovery dataset** | **NO-GO for the spatial success gate** | The administrative district category joins to rice totals descriptively. There is no validated flood footprint × dated paddy-area × district/block join and no measured flooded-rice denominator. |
| **D. Test whether recovery changes conventional rankings** | **NO-GO** | A measured recovery dimension linked to valid exposure is absent. No Phase 6 ranking was computed. |
| **E. Independently validate ranking improvement** | **NO-GO** | No held-out event/outcome with independent exposure labels and matched controls is available. |

The primary district comparison is an **exploratory association** of a flood/landslide administrative designation with 2018-19 autumn rice production, not a causal or recovery-aware prioritisation test. Do not advance to the index or manuscript conclusion on this evidence.

## Minimum outside action

The researcher should send the unsent institutional requests in [DATA_REQUESTS.md](DATA_REQUESTS.md) for the underlying NRSC/KSDMA 21 August RADARSAT-2 GIS class/footprint/QA, season-specific 2018 paddy geometry, and 2018-valid boundaries. KSDMA depth points would add independent sampled positives but require times, quality flags and a sampling frame. Nothing here requires sharing credentials or tokens. If the requested layers are released, retain their licence and metadata together with the files before any validation or exposure-area estimate.
