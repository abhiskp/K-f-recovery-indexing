## 1. NRSC/NDEM and KSDMA: independently observed inundation

**Subject:** Research access to GIS-ready RADARSAT-2 Kerala inundation, 21 August 2018 06Hr, and duration Map ID 2018/14

> We are studying whether measured post-flood agricultural outcomes change Kerala flood-priority decisions. The NRSC report for 21 August 2018 06Hr states that its RADARSAT-2 inundation layer was transmitted to KSDMA. Please provide the underlying GIS-ready raster or vector and a separate valid observed footprint (including surveyed non-flood and unobserved areas), class dictionary, exact acquisition UTC and scene ID(s), orbit/mode/polarisation, projection/resolution, processing algorithm/version, geolocation uncertainty, omissions (vegetation, urban, terrain and permanent water), QA, and the licence for academic analysis and redistribution of derived results. Please also provide the GIS-ready 11–21 August 2018 RADARSAT-2 flood-duration product (Map ID 2018/14), its per-date inputs/footprints, class thresholds and uncertainty. We will treat duration as event-hazard severity, not post-flood recovery. If direct transfer requires an agreement, please identify its form and custodian. We do not need account credentials or private keys.

Source reports: [21 August letter](https://ndem.nrsc.gov.in/documents/Disaster_Document/2018/KL/klflood50dsc21082018/klflood50dsc21082018_report.pdf); [duration PDF](https://ndem.nrsc.gov.in/documents/Disaster_Document/2018/KL/klflood50dsc11_21082018_cumulative/klflood50dsc11_21082018_cumulative_map.pdf).

## 2. Kerala DES / Agriculture Department / KSLUB or KSREC: paddy geography and boundaries

**Subject:** 2018 Kerala seasonal cultivated paddy polygons and 2018-valid administrative boundaries for research

> Please identify and, if permitted, provide georeferenced cultivated rice/paddy area for the 2018–19 autumn, winter and summer seasons, preferably actual planted/harvested field polygons or validated raster with dated mapping windows. We need the class definitions (paddy versus wetland/permanent water and other crops), observation/ground-survey method, cloud/unknown mask, positional and classification QA, projection/resolution, version, and licence. Please also provide district/block/village boundaries valid in 2018, stable unit codes and crosswalks to DES rice statistics. State whether the layer covers all paddy or only surveyed/registered fields. If no such product exists, please specify the closest dated source and its coverage. We do not request private farmer identifiers.

This request is necessary because a current wetland map or district production total cannot locate rice fields inundated in August 2018.

## 3. KSDMA/KSEOC: crowdsourced flood-depth points

**Subject:** Research-use 2018 Kerala flood-depth point dataset with date and sampling metadata

> KSDMA reports collecting about 13,000 flood-depth points after the 2018 event. Please provide, if permitted, de-identified point coordinates, observed depth and units, observation date/time versus submission date/time, survey method, location precision, quality flags, duplicate handling, accessible sampling frame/selection process, coverage limits and research-use licence. Please indicate whether dry/non-flood comparator locations were systematically sampled. We will not infer statewide precision or specificity from positive-only points.

Source: [KSDMA crowd-sourcing page](https://sdma.kerala.gov.in/crowd-sourcing-flood-2018/).
