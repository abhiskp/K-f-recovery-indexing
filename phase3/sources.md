The `.txt` files in `data/raw/` are literal tabular transcriptions of the specified official PDF pages. Spaces delimit cells, and a leading `#` denotes a comment or header. The Python script records source decisions per processed row. Hashes of the downloaded PDFs, transcriptions and outputs are in `results/file_manifest.csv`.

**Season definitions:** Kerala DES [Collection of Agricultural Statistics—Concepts and Definitions](https://www.ecostat.kerala.gov.in/storage/publications/1463.pdf) defines an agricultural year as July–June and classifies a seasonal crop by the harvest period: autumn July–October, winter November–February, summer March–June. The August 2018 flood falls within the autumn harvest window, but harvest reporting does not establish when a specific field flooded or whether a later change was caused by flooding. The compendium tables label the crop *Paddy* for area and *Rice* for production; this report preserves those original meanings. Calculated production/area is expressed as tonnes per hectare.

**2018 flood references, not digital validation data:** [KSDMA 2018 flood map](https://sdma.kerala.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/Flood_2018-1.pdf) credits NRSC; [NRSC's 21 August 2018 rapid map](https://ndem.nrsc.gov.in/documents/Disaster_Document/2018/KL/klflood50dsc21082018_1830hrs/klflood50dsc21082018_1830hrs_map.pdf) reports only partial satellite coverage. [KSDMA crowdsourced inundation depth page](https://sdma.kerala.gov.in/crowd-sourcing-flood-2018/) describes roughly 13,000 points, but a downloadable georeferenced point dataset was not obtained. [NDEM flood viewer](https://bhuvan-app1.nrsc.gov.in/disaster/usrtasks/flood/flood.php?uname=empty) advertises annual inundation layers and webservice metadata; their suitability and access for the precise 2018 pilot remain unverified. None of these maps is silently treated as ground truth.
