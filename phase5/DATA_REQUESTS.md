## Draft 1 — KSDMA and NRSC: 2018 flood GIS layers and depth observations

**To:** KSDMA/KSEOC and NRSC Disaster Management Support Group, via the above official contacts  
**Subject:** Academic research request: 2018 Kerala RADARSAT-2 GIS inundation and duration layers

Dear Sir/Madam,

We are conducting a noncommercial academic study of whether documented post-flood outcomes change Kerala flood-priority rankings. NRSC's letter No. 05-FL-2018-KL/10 states that a GIS-ready RADARSAT-2 flood layer from 21 August 2018, 0600 Hr, was transmitted to KSDMA. We request the underlying geospatial raster/vector **and its valid surveyed acquisition footprint**, not the public map PDF or styled WMS. Please provide the exact sensor scene ID and start/end time in UTC/IST; map acquisition versus issue date; CRS, resolution, class dictionary including surveyed non-flood and unobserved areas; permanent-water handling; processing/provenance; geolocation, classification and ground-check QA; and permitted research use, citation, publication of derived statistics/maps, and redistribution terms.

We also request the geospatial class/footprint layer underlying **Flood Duration Map ID 2018/14 (11–21 August 2018)**, including the four RADARSAT-2 acquisition IDs/times/footprints for 11, 14, 18 and 21 August; class codes for 0–3, 4–6, 7–9 and >10 days; the method by which duration was inferred from four discrete observations; data gaps; positional/temporal uncertainty; and licence. Its public PDF names International Charter Call ID-667, 6 March 2018 ResourceSat-2 AWiFS pre-event imagery and NRC land-cover data; please clarify those inputs' reuse and derivative-publication restrictions. We will treat this as an event hazard-severity product, not a post-flood recovery measure.

If available, please also provide the 2018 crowd-sourced depth-point table as dated georeferenced records, with depth units, observation and submission times, sampling/quality flags, location uncertainty, consent/privacy restrictions, and usage terms. A de-identified research extract is sufficient. Please indicate any application, agreement, fee or institutional approval required. We will respect restrictions and can work with access-controlled files.

Sincerely,  
[Researcher name, institution, project contact]

## Draft 2 — KSLUB and KSREC: paddy-area and season maps

**To:** Land Use Commissioner, KSLUB; Director, KSREC  
**Subject:** Academic research request: dated 2018 Kerala paddy/rice GIS layer

Dear Sir/Madam,

For a noncommercial study of 2018 flood exposure and subsequent rice outcomes, we seek a georeferenced paddy/rice extent valid **before the August 2018 flood**, preferably with 2018 autumn/winter/summer crop-season or planting/harvest status. KSLUB lists a wetland/paddy-land map; please advise its source year, scale, whether GIS files are available, and the application and fee process. A paddy-land/wetland map is not automatically a 2018 cultivated rice map, so please identify the crop definition and validation.

Please include CRS, resolution/scale, classification and unknown codes, exact reference dates, district/block identifiers, field validation/accuracy, known conversion or 2018 changes, spatial coverage, licence, attribution, and permission to publish aggregated derived results. If no 2018 season map exists, please advise the closest pre-event map and any dated agricultural land data bank or crop-survey source.

Sincerely,  
[Researcher name, institution, project contact]
