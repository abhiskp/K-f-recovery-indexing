# Source provenance and access audit

Access and screen dates: 6–8 November 2026. Local file hashes are in `results/checksums.csv`. Web links below are primary sources unless identified otherwise. No public source is treated as pixel-level validation merely because it is mapped or georeferenced.

| Source | Local evidence / access | Scientific use and limits |
|---|---|---|
| [NRSC 16 July map](https://ndem.nrsc.gov.in/documents/Disaster_Document/2018/KL/klheavyrains50dsc16072018/klheavyrains50dsc16072018_map.pdf) | Public PDF, issued 20 July. It explicitly describes a 16 July Sentinel-1A SAR flood map for part of Kerala, including Alappuzha, Ernakulam, Kottayam and Pathanamthitta. | Establishes July event chronology. Same sensor family as proposed classifier, so not independent pixel truth. PDF has a footprint legend but no usable raw label/coverage layer here. |
| [NRSC 9 August report](https://ndem.nrsc.gov.in/documents/Disaster_Document/2018/KL/klheavyrains50dsc09082018/klheavyrains50dsc09082018_report.pdf) and [IIRS page](https://www.iirs.gov.in/iirs_slide_page?s=42) | Public report/slide; Sentinel-1 flood observations on 9 and 21 August, later imagery on 27 August and 2 September discussed. | Confirms multiple flood dates; exact acquisition metadata must come from the scene inventory. |
| [NRSC 21 August 06Hr report](https://ndem.nrsc.gov.in/documents/Disaster_Document/2018/KL/klflood50dsc21082018/klflood50dsc21082018_report.pdf) | Public PDF. It states Radarsat-2 21 August 06Hr mapped part of Kerala and a GIS-ready flood layer was sent to KSDMA. Printed total is 36,658 ha, but its five district rows sum to 36,657 ha; Alappuzha and Ernakulam were only partly covered. The exact transcription and arithmetic check are retained. Its table lists earlier Sentinel-1, Radarsat-2, ALOS-2, and TerraSAR-X events. | Best identified independent-sensor geospatial lead. The 1 ha source discrepancy is not silently corrected. The PDF's area total is a partial-coverage operational statistic, not statewide flood area or pixel labels. KSDMA/NRSC GIS file, footprint, coding, licence and assessment metadata are still required. |
| [NRSC 21 August 18Hr map](https://ndem.nrsc.gov.in/documents/Disaster_Document/2018/KL/klflood50dsc21082018_1830hrs/klflood50dsc21082018_1830hrs_map.pdf) | Public PDF, Sentinel-1A plus Radarsat-2; explicitly no ground verification. | Cross-source visual context only. It is partly statistically dependent on Sentinel-1. |
| [Bhuvan flood viewer](https://bhuvan-app1.nrsc.gov.in/disaster/usrtasks/flood/flood.php?uname=empty) / [WMS endpoint](https://bhuvan-gp1.nrsc.gov.in/bhuvan/wms) | Raw viewer HTML, parsed 16 Kerala 2018 WMS layers, and 3 dated pilot `GetMap` PNG/GeoTIFF responses are preserved locally. `GetCapabilities` 1.3 and 1.1.1 responses had malformed XML and translator errors. The 21 Aug 06Hr Radarsat-2 layer is listed but its GeoTIFF and PNG `GetMap` requests both returned `ServiceException java.lang.NullPointerException`; the response is saved. | Styled WMS views show water colors and watermark; no raw binary class key, no per-pixel acquisition footprint, no validated negatives. Viewer zoom boxes are not footprints. Date discrepancy: 9 Aug label vs `kl_2018_10_08`; hour discrepancy: 21 Jul 06Hr label vs `_18`. These require resolution before exact date matching. |
| [KSDMA 2018 crowd-sourcing page](https://sdma.kerala.gov.in/crowd-sourcing-flood-2018/) and [PDF](https://sdma.kerala.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/CROWD_INUNDATION.pdf) | Page reports roughly 13,000 inundation/depth observations; publicly visible download is a PDF, not an observation table. | Request licensed georeferenced points with coordinates, observation date/time, water depth/units, survey/quality method, sampling frame, positional uncertainty, and permitted research use. A report figure cannot support unbiased confusion metrics. |
| [Earth Engine Sentinel-1 GRD catalog](https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/COPERNICUS_S1_GRD) and [JRC annual water history](https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/JRC_GSW1_4_YearlyHistory) | Authenticated Code Editor pilot query executed; 134 IW/VV intersecting granules, complete CSV copied locally. The 51 granules in 2018 form 27 acquisitions. Six S1A orbit-165 acquisitions were selected for separate 9/21 August exploratory comparisons. The original six-row QA CSV and corrected 45-band GeoTIFF are now preserved in data/raw; their task IDs and failed first export are recorded. Local raster QA found JRC history finite over only 36.53% of the pilot and strict support over 23.57%. JRC 2014–17 annual classes are used only for a pre-event permanent-water screen. | GRD backscatter and incidence angle are candidate exposure evidence only. Reference dry status is unverified; the 16 July scene is never in the reference. The 9/21 August candidate masks are neither independent labels nor validated flood extent. |
| [Landsat 8 Collection 2 L2](https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/LANDSAT_LC08_C02_T1_L2), [Sentinel-2 SR](https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/COPERNICUS_S2_SR_HARMONIZED), [Sentinel-2 TOA](https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/COPERNICUS_S2_HARMONIZED) | Eight pilot-month image counts and QA-based availability fractions were computed in Earth Engine and saved in `results/pilot_2017_2018_optical_viirs_feasibility.csv`. Official Sentinel-2 SR catalog warns 2017–18 L2 coverage was not global. | No 2017/2018 monsoon SR scenes in the pilot; TOA exists but QA60 cloud-only flags omit shadows and may overstate usable vegetation coverage. Landsat August 2018 clear-any fraction was 0.00193. |
| [VIIRS monthly VCMSLCFG](https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/NOAA_VIIRS_DNB_MONTHLY_V1_VCMSLCFG) | Eight monthly pilot `cf_cvg` and `avg_rad` means computed; one composite per month. | `cf_cvg` is the count of cloud-free observations per pixel; 2018 July/August means are 0.113/0.353, too sparse for a direct economic-recovery inference. The product does not filter temporary lights or separate lights from background. |
| [GFD v1 catalog](https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/GLOBAL_FLOOD_DB_MODIS_EVENTS_V1), [producer QC metadata](https://github.com/cloudtostreet/MODIS_GlobalFloodDatabase), [public bucket listing](https://storage.googleapis.com/storage/v1/b/gfd_v1_4/o?maxResults=1000&fields=items(name,size,md5Hash,mediaLink),nextPageToken) | Complete 913-object catalog and 913-row QC snapshot saved. All 32 events beginning 2018 were bbox-screened; none intersects the broad Kerala screen bbox. | No matching 2018 Kerala event. GFD uses MODIS 250 m inputs even though some display/export is 30 m; no GFD raster is used as a 30 m validation label. |
| [Open Data Kerala district repo](https://github.com/opendatakerala/kl_district) / [GeoJSON](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/opendatakerala/kl_district/main/Kerala_districts.geojson) | 845 mixed raw features; filtered to 14 district Polygon/MultiPolygon features. OSM-derived, ODbL attribution required. | Candidate join geometry only. Snapshot has later edits and has not been verified against 2018 administrative boundaries. |
| [Phase 3 official DES rice panel](../phase3/sources.md) | 252 district-season-year rows, 14 districts × 3 seasons × agricultural years 2014–15 to 2019–20. | Valid outcome totals (area ha, production tonnes, yield tonnes/ha) at district-season scale, subject to Phase 3 corrections. No inference of pixel recovery, flood association or duration without exposure join and controls. |
| [DES 2020–21](https://www.ecostat.kerala.gov.in/storage/publications/622.pdf), [2021–22](https://www.ecostat.kerala.gov.in/storage/publications/664.pdf), [2022–23](https://ecostat.kerala.gov.in/storage/publications/1720.pdf) | Longer official annual reports located, not transcribed or analyzed in Phase 4. | Potential later district-season extension; source definition, district units, other floods and weather trends require harmonization. No recovery result is claimed from them. |
| [KSREC paddy mapping](https://ksrec.kerala.gov.in/website/website_english/HomeController/ongoing_projects), [KSLUB wetland system](https://kslub.kerala.gov.in/en/wetland-information-system-2024-25/?lang=en), [FASAL](https://desagri.gov.in/programs-schemes/forecasting-agricultural-output-using-space-agro-meteorology-and-land-based-observations-fasal/) | Programs located; no publicly downloadable, verified 2018 paddy polygon/raster with crop-year definition found. | Candidate institutional routes for georeferenced rice area; no affected-rice-area calculation yet. |

For KSDMA/NRSC data requests, ask for the 21 August 06Hr GIS-ready inundation vector/raster and the crowd depth observations, with observation and product dates separated. Ask for full source acquisition IDs, study footprint/validity mask, class codes, spatial accuracy/uncertainty, processing provenance and redistribution licence. No credentials or tokens are needed in the project folder.
