# Phase 4: 2018 exposure evidence and validation gate

This folder preserves Phases 2 and 3 and addresses the first spatial evidence gate. It is **not** a recovery-aware ranking or a validated flood extent.

## Executed findings (6–8 November 2026)

- Official NRSC mapped flooding from a **16 July 2018 Sentinel-1A** acquisition. July 2018 is therefore not an automatically dry pre-event window. The [chronology](data/processed/official_event_chronology.csv) records later sensor dates and source-specific uncertainty.
- A signed-in Earth Engine query returned **134 Sentinel-1 IW/VV granules** intersecting the pilot rectangle (76.35–76.60°E, 9.45–9.75°N) during 1 June 2016–14 October 2018 UTC. Its full metadata CSV export completed in task `LMCGG75TSBJP6WZWRKV3MTZ2` and the actual CSV is now in [data/raw/](data/raw/). Of **51 granules in 2018**, only **27 are distinct dated acquisitions**: 24 comprise two adjacent granules and three are single-granule observations. The [acquisition groups](results/gee_2018_acquisition_groups.csv) and [fixed pilot selection](results/selected_2018_pilot_acquisitions.csv) preserve exact scene IDs, UTC/local times, orbit, angle, and validity. No reference date is accepted as independently verified dry.
- The public Bhuvan viewer lists **16 Kerala 2018 WMS layers**. Three dated pilot WMS GeoTIFFs and PNGs were downloaded. They are *styled visualizations* with no per-pixel observation footprint or binary class key. The 9 August viewer label conflicts with its layer ID (`kl_2018_10_08`); a 21 July 06Hr label conflicts with its `_18` layer suffix.
- The listed 21 August 06Hr Radarsat-2 WMS layer failed `GetMap` in both GeoTIFF and PNG with `ServiceException java.lang.NullPointerException`; the exact public error response is retained. This further prevents treating the public viewer as an accessible independent label dataset.
- NRSC's Radarsat-2 21 August 06Hr report lists five district inundation areas adding to **36,657 ha**, while its printed total is **36,658 ha**; Alappuzha and Ernakulam are explicitly only partly covered. The 1 ha arithmetic discrepancy and coverage qualification are retained in a transcribed QA table.
- Screening all **913** public Global Flood Database v1 objects against its QC polygon metadata found **32** events beginning in 2018 and **zero** whose bounding box intersects a broad Kerala search box. GFD therefore supplies no matching 2018 Kerala event for this analysis.
- A public OSM-derived snapshot yields 14 candidate district polygons, but its geometry is not certified as 2018 boundaries. No verified 2018 georeferenced paddy extent or independent, dated pixel labels have been obtained.
- A separate executed 8-month pilot availability check found **zero Sentinel-2 SR scenes** in June–September 2017/2018, though Sentinel-2 TOA scenes existed. Landsat 8 had **zero July 2018 scenes** and only **0.19%** of pilot pixels passed the conservative August clear-any screen. Mean VIIRS cloud-free observations per pixel were **0.113 in July** and **0.353 in August 2018**. These are observation-quality results, not ecological or economic recovery estimates.
- A second Earth Engine run checked six matched S1A descending orbit-165 acquisitions: 10/22 June and 4 July reference candidates, 16 July chronology check, and separate 9/21 August event candidates. It mosaics adjacent granules before comparing dates. The [six-row QA CSV](data/raw/phase4_s1_2018_pilot_selected_acquisition_qa.csv) and [45-band GeoTIFF](data/raw/phase4_s1_2018_pilot_exploratory_candidate_bands.tif) are now local. Their exports completed under tasks `3RLMJCJNATOXPRLQ4MPHD3KI` and `XZNENJ6GNT6VN5W7X6F2FKMB`. Their candidate masks are **not** flood extent without independent labels.
- The selected descending acquisitions begin about **06:10 IST**. The exported QA CSV exactly matches the six console-transcribed counts, coverage fractions and angle means; its scene/orbit IDs match the full inventory. Approximate valid VV coverage is **99.8946–99.8967%**; mean incidence angles are **40.7739–40.7812°** (range 0.0073°). A separate 21 August S1A ascending acquisition begins **18:36 IST**, has a different orbit (173) and angle (~36.73°), and is excluded. The 21 August NRSC 06Hr Radarsat-2 product is only approximately contemporaneous with the morning S1A scene; its exact source UTC/footprint remain unavailable.
- The local raster QA found that JRC 2014–17 water history has finite values for only **36.53%** of the pilot, and the strict SAR candidate support after water/slope checks is **23.57%**. Under the unvalidated early-reference 3 dB rule, **60,958** pixels on 9 August and **128,003** on 21 August are candidate-positive within that support (25.56% and 53.68% of support). These are **classification counts, not inundated area**. The export has no explicit nodata value: some analytic bands contain NaNs, while candidate-mask zeros include pixels outside support. See [raster QA](results/gee_export_validation.json), [threshold table](results/sar_candidate_pixel_sensitivity.csv) and [figure](figures/2018_pilot_sar_support_and_candidates.png).

The executed outputs are in [`data/processed/`](data/processed/) and [`results/`](results/). The source screening command is `python3 phase4/scripts/screen_public_sources.py` from the project root. See [REPRODUCE.md](REPRODUCE.md) and [sources.md](sources.md).

## Scientific status

**Gate: NO-GO** for validated inundation, affected rice area, flood-attributable crop recovery, or improved rankings. The Phase 3 official rice panel remains a seasonal district-level outcome series, not a pixel-level crop recovery map. The public 21 August Radarsat-2 NRSC report identifies a promising independent-sensor GIS layer transmitted to KSDMA, but that layer and its licence, footprint and class/accuracy metadata are not locally available. See [decision_gate.md](decision_gate.md), [qa_validation.md](qa_validation.md), and [methods_and_limitations.md](methods_and_limitations.md).

## Folder contents

- `data/raw`: original public page/catalog/QC/boundary downloads and three dated **styled** WMS pilot views.
- `data/processed`: parsed source inventory, WMS technical metadata, event chronology, and candidate district geometry.
- `results`: executed source-screen and acquisition-inventory tables and status. No mask-accuracy or recovery-effect result exists yet.
- `results/pilot_2017_2018_optical_viirs_feasibility.csv`: eight actual Earth Engine month-level feasibility observations, copied from the Code Editor's computed feature table and independently range/shape checked.
- `results/gee_export_validation.json`, `sar_candidate_pixel_sensitivity.csv` and `sar_band_diagnostics.csv`: technical raster QA and descriptive unvalidated candidate counts from the actual 45-band GeoTIFF.
- `figures/2018_pilot_acquisition_chronology.svg`: vector timeline from the local Earth Engine inventory and dated NRSC products, with reference-date uncertainty explicit.
- `figures/2018_pilot_sar_support_and_candidates.png` and `.pdf`: publication-ready QA/sensitivity figure, expressly not a flood map or exposure estimate.
- `scripts`: standard-library-only source screening and checksum manifest generation.
- `gee_scene_inventory_EXECUTED.js`, `gee_other_domain_feasibility.js`, and `gee_candidate_masks_EXECUTED.js`: executed Code Editor source. `gee_candidate_masks_UNEXECUTED.js` is the reproduction template; its `START_EXPORTS` gate is off. `gee_candidate_masks_INITIAL_FAILED.js` preserves the first mixed-band-type export failure; `gee_candidate_masks_OBSOLETE_UNEXECUTED.js` preserves a superseded template that incorrectly counted adjacent granules as independent dates. Neither should be used for analysis.

No credentials, tokens, private keys, or hidden account configuration are stored here.
